# Translation log

One entry per function translated from asm to C: function, method, how it was verified, known differences.

**mips2c v0** (`tools/mips2c/mips2c.py`, M1): one C function per `.s` file, every glabel an entry; registers as locals, `$sp` on the emulated stack in the arena; every load and store maps the KUSEG RAM mirror (0x00000000-0x001FFFFF) onto the arena; computed jumps dispatch on PS1 code addresses. "Ran without fault" means no crash, no `mips2c_bad_jump` and no PsyCross parse error in `logs/run1-final3` (title and menu, 2600 vblanks) and `logs/run1-demo4` (12000 vblanks, two attract demos). It is not proof of equivalence. "Differential test" means `tests/m2c_diff` replayed calls captured in PCSX-Redux (porting-plan §7.3, decision D22) against the translation and compared registers, GTE, scratchpad and memory.

**M2 status (2026-10-29, before the oracle; superseded below):** no function was verified against the emulator yet, as PCSX-Redux was waiting for download approval. What M2 adds is gameplay coverage on the host, which exercises the translated pete, camera, collision, renderer and gamestate code in Artisans: walking, charge, flame, jump and glide, freeing Nestor (dragon cutscene), the save fairy and "GAME SAVED", pause, options and inventory, respawn and game over, plus gem collection in both attract demos (logs `run3-*` to `run6-*`, listed in `docs/host-build.md`). One translation-environment bug was found and fixed this way: `r_environment.s` tests the sign bit of a pointer into `D_8006FCF4`, which only works with the game's data at its PS1 addresses (decisions D23). "Ran without fault" below now also covers these runs.

**Oracle status (2026-10-30):** PCSX-Redux runs (decisions D8). The "Verified by" column of 98 translated entry points now reads "Oracle replay": every sampled call was replayed through the translated C from the emulator's entry state (registers, RAM, scratchpad, GTE) and matched the emulator's exit state (948 of 948 samples; `logs/run8-oracle/cap3` boot, title and both attract demos; `logs/run8-oracle/cap5-fairy` new game, freeing Nestor, the save fairy; `logs/run8-oracle/cap6-tour` the pete and camera functions in 30 levels, reached with the level-warp cheat). What isn't compared is listed with reasons in `tools/oracle/ignore_ranges.txt` (interrupt-written state, the asm spill area, libcd DMA, dead return registers); how the replay works is in `src/game/oracle_replay.c`. M1's runner (decisions D25) adds `tests/m2c_diff` differential cases for `math.s` and `tests/gte`, where PsyCross's GTE matches PCSX-Redux bit for bit on 9,314 vectors; rows that have both say "Also differential test". The 47 rows still without a replay are mostly internal labels (`D_…`, entered by jumps, not calls), functions these runs don't call, and the credits overlay. Both attract demos also match the emulator tick for tick on every traced field (Stone Hill 866 ticks, Doctor Shemp 768).

**M3 Vita (2026-10-30):** the first Vita build found one more translation-environment bug, the same kind as D23's: three renderers build OT tags as `(len << 24 | 0x80000000) ^ next_packet`, which leaves `len` only when the arena's top byte is 0x80. On the Vita (arena at 0x89000000) the length came out 9 too big and PsyCross rejected the packets (sky, shadows, particles). The tag constants in those units, `r_cyclorama.s` (0x80/0x84/0x86000000, 12 sites), `r_shadows.s` (0x82000000) and `r_particles.s` (0x83000000), are now emitted as `MIPS_ARENA_TAG(k)` (mips2c hint `arena_tags`, decisions D25); on linux32 the macro is the constant itself. Verified by: no PsyCross error in 12000 vblanks of title and attract demos in Vita3K, and every screenshot byte-identical to the linux32 host's (`logs/run2-vita-title`, `logs/run4-vita-demo`; docs/vita-testing.md).

**Whole-game status (2026-10-30, `docs/whole-game-host.md`):** the same level tour ran in PCSX-Redux and on the host (decisions D31): every level 10-64, both endings with their cutscenes and credits, the credits cheat. Two captures of it, `logs/run14-oracle-tour` and `logs/run19-oracle-labels`, replay 3,707 of 3,707 completed samples matching (3 more never return, below), with up to one sample per function per level and six per credits function. Of the 47 rows that had no sample after M2: 12 callable functions now have matching samples (the credits overlay's two among them); 25 are labels entered by computed jumps inside another function, so they can't be called: 7 of those ran inside matching samples of their function, 4 ran but never during a completed sample, 14 never ran in the tour; 4 have no caller anywhere (dead code); 6 weren't called in the tour (the level-exit and balloonist gamestates, which warps skip, one cyclorama branch, two rare pete/moby paths). Fixes from this run are under "Whole-game additions" at the end.
